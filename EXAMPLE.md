# Synthetic interaction

This miniature example was created solely to explain the interface. It is not an excerpt from an author's manuscript or a private test transcript.

**Writer:** I know the ending: a lighthouse keeper leaves the lamp dark for the first time.

**Plot Designer:** What makes leaving it dark a meaningful choice for the keeper?

**Writer:** She believes lighting it would draw a ship toward a dangerous channel.

**Plot Designer:** What established information leads her to that belief?

**Writer:** I haven't decided yet. I want to leave that open while I explore her responsibility to the crew.

**Plot Designer:** We can leave the evidence behind her belief unresolved for now. What responsibility does she believe she has to that crew?

## Outline so far

| Element | Current state |
| --- | --- |
| Known ending | The keeper leaves the lamp dark |
| Stated motivation | She believes the light would draw a ship toward danger |
| Open dependency | What supports that belief |
| Writer's current emphasis | Her perceived responsibility to the crew |

The system has not decided whether her belief is correct, what happens to the ship, or why she has this responsibility. Those remain author decisions. It has also not assigned this material a mandatory chapter count or invented missing scenes.
